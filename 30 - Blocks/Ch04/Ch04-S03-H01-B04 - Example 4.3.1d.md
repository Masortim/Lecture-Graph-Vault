# Orthogonal Projection Example 4.3.1d

**正交投影例子 4.3.1d** · example · 4.3.1d

⬆️ Part of [[Ch04-S03-H01 - Definition Orthogonal Projection|Definition: Orthogonal Projection]] · [[Ch04-S03-H01 - Definition Orthogonal Projection|正交投影：定义]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。

Used together with: [[Ch07-S04-H06-B02 - Estimate 7.4.6b|Estimate 7.4.6b]], [[Ch09-S01-H05-B02 - Identity 9.1.5b|Identity 9.1.5b]].
