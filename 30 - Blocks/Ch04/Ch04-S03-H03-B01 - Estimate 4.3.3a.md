# Dual Space Estimate 4.3.3a

**对偶空间估计 4.3.3a** · estimate · 4.3.3a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch04-S03-H03 - Basic Properties Dual Space|Basic Properties: Dual Space]] · [[Ch04-S03-H03 - Basic Properties Dual Space|对偶空间：基本性质]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。

Used together with: [[Ch08-S02-H02-B02 - Estimate 8.2.2b|Estimate 8.2.2b]], [[Ch09-S03-H01-B02 - Identity 9.3.1b|Identity 9.3.1b]].
