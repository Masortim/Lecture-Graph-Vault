# Generalization Bound Example 4.1.6c

**泛化界例子 4.1.6c** · example · 4.1.6c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch04-S01-H06 - Proof and Consequences Generalization Bound|Proof and Consequences: Generalization Bound]] · [[Ch04-S01-H06 - Proof and Consequences Generalization Bound|泛化界：证明与推论]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。
