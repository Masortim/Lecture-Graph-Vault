# Contraction Mapping Proposition 4.4.1a

**压缩映射命题 4.4.1a** · statement · 4.4.1a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch04-S04-H01 - Definition Contraction Mapping|Definition: Contraction Mapping]] · [[Ch04-S04-H01 - Definition Contraction Mapping|压缩映射：定义]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。
