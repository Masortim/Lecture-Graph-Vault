# Spectral Theorem Proposition 4.3.5a

**谱定理命题 4.3.5a** · statement · 4.3.5a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch04-S03-H05 - Main Theorem Spectral Theorem|Main Theorem: Spectral Theorem]] · [[Ch04-S03-H05 - Main Theorem Spectral Theorem|谱定理：主定理]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。

Used together with: [[Ch08-S04-H04-B02 - Proposition 8.4.4b|Proposition 8.4.4b]], [[Ch01-S01-H03-B02 - Example 1.1.3b|Example 1.1.3b]].
