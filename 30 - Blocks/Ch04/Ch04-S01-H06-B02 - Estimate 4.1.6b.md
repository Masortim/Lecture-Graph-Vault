# Generalization Bound Estimate 4.1.6b

**泛化界估计 4.1.6b** · estimate · 4.1.6b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch04-S01-H06 - Proof and Consequences Generalization Bound|Proof and Consequences: Generalization Bound]] · [[Ch04-S01-H06 - Proof and Consequences Generalization Bound|泛化界：证明与推论]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。

Used together with: [[Ch05-S03-H06-B03 - Example 5.3.6c|Example 5.3.6c]], [[Ch06-S04-H05-B03 - Estimate 6.4.5c|Estimate 6.4.5c]].
