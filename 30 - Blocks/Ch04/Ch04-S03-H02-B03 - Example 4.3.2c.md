# Basis and Coordinates Example 4.3.2c

**基与坐标例子 4.3.2c** · example · 4.3.2c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch04-S03-H02 - Notation Basis and Coordinates|Notation: Basis and Coordinates]] · [[Ch04-S03-H02 - Notation Basis and Coordinates|基与坐标：记号约定]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。

Used together with: [[Ch08-S01-H06-B02 - Estimate 8.1.6b|Estimate 8.1.6b]], [[Ch09-S02-H05-B02 - Identity 9.2.5b|Identity 9.2.5b]].
