# Dual Space Example 4.3.3b

**对偶空间例子 4.3.3b** · example · 4.3.3b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch04-S03-H03 - Basic Properties Dual Space|Basic Properties: Dual Space]] · [[Ch04-S03-H03 - Basic Properties Dual Space|对偶空间：基本性质]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。
