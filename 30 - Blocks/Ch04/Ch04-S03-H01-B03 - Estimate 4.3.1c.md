# Orthogonal Projection Estimate 4.3.1c

**正交投影估计 4.3.1c** · estimate · 4.3.1c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch04-S03-H01 - Definition Orthogonal Projection|Definition: Orthogonal Projection]] · [[Ch04-S03-H01 - Definition Orthogonal Projection|正交投影：定义]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。

Used together with: [[Ch07-S04-H04-B02 - Proposition 7.4.4b|Proposition 7.4.4b]], [[Ch09-S01-H03-B02 - Example 9.1.3b|Example 9.1.3b]].
