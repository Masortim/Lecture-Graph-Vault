# Adjoint Operator Proposition 4.3.4b

**伴随算子命题 4.3.4b** · statement · 4.3.4b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch04-S03-H04 - Key Lemma Adjoint Operator|Key Lemma: Adjoint Operator]] · [[Ch04-S03-H04 - Key Lemma Adjoint Operator|伴随算子：关键引理]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。
