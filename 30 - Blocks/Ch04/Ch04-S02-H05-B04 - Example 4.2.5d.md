# Compact Set Example 4.2.5d

**紧集例子 4.2.5d** · example · 4.2.5d

⬆️ Part of [[Ch04-S02-H05 - Main Theorem Compact Set|Main Theorem: Compact Set]] · [[Ch04-S02-H05 - Main Theorem Compact Set|紧集：主定理]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。

Used together with: [[Ch07-S02-H03-B03 - Proposition 7.2.3c|Proposition 7.2.3c]], [[Ch08-S03-H02-B02 - Estimate 8.3.2b|Estimate 8.3.2b]].
