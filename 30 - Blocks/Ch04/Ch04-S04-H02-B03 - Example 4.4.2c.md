# Fixed Point Example 4.4.2c

**不动点例子 4.4.2c** · example · 4.4.2c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch04-S04-H02 - Notation Fixed Point|Notation: Fixed Point]] · [[Ch04-S04-H02 - Notation Fixed Point|不动点：记号约定]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。

Used together with: [[Ch01-S01-H01-B02 - Identity 1.1.1b|Identity 1.1.1b]], [[Ch02-S01-H06-B01 - Identity 2.1.6a|Identity 2.1.6a]].
