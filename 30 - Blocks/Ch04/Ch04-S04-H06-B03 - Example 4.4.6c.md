# Density Argument Example 4.4.6c

**稠密性论证例子 4.4.6c** · example · 4.4.6c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch04-S04-H06 - Proof and Consequences Density Argument|Proof and Consequences: Density Argument]] · [[Ch04-S04-H06 - Proof and Consequences Density Argument|稠密性论证：证明与推论]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。
