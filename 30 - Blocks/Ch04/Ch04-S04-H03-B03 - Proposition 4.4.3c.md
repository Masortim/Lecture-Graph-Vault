# Banach Limit Proposition 4.4.3c

**巴拿赫极限命题 4.4.3c** · statement · 4.4.3c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch04-S04-H03 - Basic Properties Banach Limit|Basic Properties: Banach Limit]] · [[Ch04-S04-H03 - Basic Properties Banach Limit|巴拿赫极限：基本性质]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。
