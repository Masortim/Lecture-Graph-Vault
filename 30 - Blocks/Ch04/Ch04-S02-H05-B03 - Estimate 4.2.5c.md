# Compact Set Estimate 4.2.5c

**紧集估计 4.2.5c** · estimate · 4.2.5c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch04-S02-H05 - Main Theorem Compact Set|Main Theorem: Compact Set]] · [[Ch04-S02-H05 - Main Theorem Compact Set|紧集：主定理]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。
