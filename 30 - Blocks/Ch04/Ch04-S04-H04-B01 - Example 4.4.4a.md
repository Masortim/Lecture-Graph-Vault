# Hilbert Decomposition Example 4.4.4a

**希尔伯特分解例子 4.4.4a** · example · 4.4.4a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch04-S04-H04 - Key Lemma Hilbert Decomposition|Key Lemma: Hilbert Decomposition]] · [[Ch04-S04-H04 - Key Lemma Hilbert Decomposition|希尔伯特分解：关键引理]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。
