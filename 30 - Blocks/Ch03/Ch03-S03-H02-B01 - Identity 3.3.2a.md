# Separation Theorem Identity 3.3.2a

**分离定理恒等式 3.3.2a** · identity · 3.3.2a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch03-S03-H02 - Notation Separation Theorem|Notation: Separation Theorem]] · [[Ch03-S03-H02 - Notation Separation Theorem|分离定理：记号约定]]

The pairing is computed in coordinates, which gives the identity we quote later:

$$
\langle x, y \rangle \;=\; \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i \overline{y_i}
 \;=\; \lVert P_{M} x \rVert^{2} + \lVert P_{M^{\perp}} x \rVert^{2}.
$$

恒等式在正交投影的讨论中反复使用。
