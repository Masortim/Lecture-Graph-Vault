# Lipschitz Continuity Estimate 3.4.6b

**利普希茨连续性估计 3.4.6b** · estimate · 3.4.6b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch03-S04-H06 - Proof and Consequences Lipschitz Continuity|Proof and Consequences: Lipschitz Continuity]] · [[Ch03-S04-H06 - Proof and Consequences Lipschitz Continuity|利普希茨连续性：证明与推论]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。

Used together with: [[Ch04-S01-H03-B02 - Example 4.1.3b|Example 4.1.3b]], [[Ch05-S02-H02-B01 - Identity 5.2.2a|Identity 5.2.2a]].
