# Uniform Boundedness Identity 3.3.6a

**一致有界性恒等式 3.3.6a** · identity · 3.3.6a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch03-S03-H06 - Proof and Consequences Uniform Boundedness|Proof and Consequences: Uniform Boundedness]] · [[Ch03-S03-H06 - Proof and Consequences Uniform Boundedness|一致有界性：证明与推论]]

The pairing is computed in coordinates, which gives the identity we quote later:

$$
\langle x, y \rangle \;=\; \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i \overline{y_i}
 \;=\; \lVert P_{M} x \rVert^{2} + \lVert P_{M^{\perp}} x \rVert^{2}.
$$

恒等式在正交投影的讨论中反复使用。
