# Approximation Error Example 3.4.1d

**逼近误差例子 3.4.1d** · example · 3.4.1d

⬆️ Part of [[Ch03-S04-H01 - Definition Approximation Error|Definition: Approximation Error]] · [[Ch03-S04-H01 - Definition Approximation Error|逼近误差：定义]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。
