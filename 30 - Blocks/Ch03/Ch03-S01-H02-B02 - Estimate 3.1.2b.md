# Norm Equivalence Estimate 3.1.2b

**范数等价估计 3.1.2b** · estimate · 3.1.2b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch03-S01-H02 - Notation Norm Equivalence|Notation: Norm Equivalence]] · [[Ch03-S01-H02 - Notation Norm Equivalence|范数等价：记号约定]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。

Used together with: [[Ch06-S03-H02-B03 - Example 6.3.2c|Example 6.3.2c]], [[Ch07-S04-H01-B03 - Estimate 7.4.1c|Estimate 7.4.1c]].
