# Banach Limit Proposition 3.1.5a

**巴拿赫极限命题 3.1.5a** · statement · 3.1.5a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch03-S01-H05 - Main Theorem Banach Limit|Main Theorem: Banach Limit]] · [[Ch03-S01-H05 - Main Theorem Banach Limit|巴拿赫极限：主定理]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。
