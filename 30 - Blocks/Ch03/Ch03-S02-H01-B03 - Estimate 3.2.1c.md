# Weak Convergence Estimate 3.2.1c

**弱收敛估计 3.2.1c** · estimate · 3.2.1c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch03-S02-H01 - Definition Weak Convergence|Definition: Weak Convergence]] · [[Ch03-S02-H01 - Definition Weak Convergence|弱收敛：定义]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。
