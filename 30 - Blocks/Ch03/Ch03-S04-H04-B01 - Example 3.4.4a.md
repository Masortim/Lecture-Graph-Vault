# Concentration Inequality Example 3.4.4a

**集中不等式例子 3.4.4a** · example · 3.4.4a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch03-S04-H04 - Key Lemma Concentration Inequality|Key Lemma: Concentration Inequality]] · [[Ch03-S04-H04 - Key Lemma Concentration Inequality|集中不等式：关键引理]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。

Used together with: [[Ch03-S02-H05-B02 - Identity 3.2.5b|Identity 3.2.5b]], [[Ch04-S03-H04-B01 - Example 4.3.4a|Example 4.3.4a]].
