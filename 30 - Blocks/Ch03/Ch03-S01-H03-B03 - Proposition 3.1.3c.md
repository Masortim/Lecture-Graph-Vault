# Contraction Mapping Proposition 3.1.3c

**压缩映射命题 3.1.3c** · statement · 3.1.3c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch03-S01-H03 - Basic Properties Contraction Mapping|Basic Properties: Contraction Mapping]] · [[Ch03-S01-H03 - Basic Properties Contraction Mapping|压缩映射：基本性质]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。

Used together with: [[Ch06-S04-H04-B03 - Identity 6.4.4c|Identity 6.4.4c]], [[Ch08-S01-H03-B03 - Proposition 8.1.3c|Proposition 8.1.3c]].
