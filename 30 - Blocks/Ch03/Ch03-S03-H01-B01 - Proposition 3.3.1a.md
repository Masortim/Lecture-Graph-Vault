# Convex Hull Proposition 3.3.1a

**凸包命题 3.3.1a** · statement · 3.3.1a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch03-S03-H01 - Definition Convex Hull|Definition: Convex Hull]] · [[Ch03-S03-H01 - Definition Convex Hull|凸包：定义]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。
