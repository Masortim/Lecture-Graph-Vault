# Open Mapping Identity 3.3.4c

**开映射恒等式 3.3.4c** · identity · 3.3.4c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch03-S03-H04 - Key Lemma Open Mapping|Key Lemma: Open Mapping]] · [[Ch03-S03-H04 - Key Lemma Open Mapping|开映射：关键引理]]

The pairing is computed in coordinates, which gives the identity we quote later:

$$
\langle x, y \rangle \;=\; \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i \overline{y_i}
 \;=\; \lVert P_{M} x \rVert^{2} + \lVert P_{M^{\perp}} x \rVert^{2}.
$$

恒等式在正交投影的讨论中反复使用。
