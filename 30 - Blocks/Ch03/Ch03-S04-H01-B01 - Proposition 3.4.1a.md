# Approximation Error Proposition 3.4.1a

**逼近误差命题 3.4.1a** · statement · 3.4.1a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch03-S04-H01 - Definition Approximation Error|Definition: Approximation Error]] · [[Ch03-S04-H01 - Definition Approximation Error|逼近误差：定义]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。
