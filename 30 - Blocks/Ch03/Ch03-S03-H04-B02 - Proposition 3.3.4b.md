# Open Mapping Proposition 3.3.4b

**开映射命题 3.3.4b** · statement · 3.3.4b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch03-S03-H04 - Key Lemma Open Mapping|Key Lemma: Open Mapping]] · [[Ch03-S03-H04 - Key Lemma Open Mapping|开映射：关键引理]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。
