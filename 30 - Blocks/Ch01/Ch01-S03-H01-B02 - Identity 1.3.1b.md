# Banach Limit Identity 1.3.1b

**巴拿赫极限恒等式 1.3.1b** · identity · 1.3.1b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch01-S03-H01 - Definition Banach Limit|Definition: Banach Limit]] · [[Ch01-S03-H01 - Definition Banach Limit|巴拿赫极限：定义]]

The pairing is computed in coordinates, which gives the identity we quote later:

$$
\langle x, y \rangle \;=\; \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i \overline{y_i}
 \;=\; \lVert P_{M} x \rVert^{2} + \lVert P_{M^{\perp}} x \rVert^{2}.
$$

恒等式在正交投影的讨论中反复使用。
