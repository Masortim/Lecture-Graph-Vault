# Convex Hull Estimate 1.4.3a

**凸包估计 1.4.3a** · estimate · 1.4.3a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch01-S04-H03 - Basic Properties Convex Hull|Basic Properties: Convex Hull]] · [[Ch01-S04-H03 - Basic Properties Convex Hull|凸包：基本性质]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。

Used together with: [[Ch07-S01-H03-B02 - Example 7.1.3b|Example 7.1.3b]], [[Ch08-S02-H02-B01 - Identity 8.2.2a|Identity 8.2.2a]].
