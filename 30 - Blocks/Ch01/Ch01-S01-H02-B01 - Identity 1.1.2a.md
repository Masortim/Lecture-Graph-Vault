# Linear Operator Identity 1.1.2a

**线性算子恒等式 1.1.2a** · identity · 1.1.2a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch01-S01-H02 - Notation Linear Operator|Notation: Linear Operator]] · [[Ch01-S01-H02 - Notation Linear Operator|线性算子：记号约定]]

The pairing is computed in coordinates, which gives the identity we quote later:

$$
\langle x, y \rangle \;=\; \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i \overline{y_i}
 \;=\; \lVert P_{M} x \rVert^{2} + \lVert P_{M^{\perp}} x \rVert^{2}.
$$

恒等式在正交投影的讨论中反复使用。
