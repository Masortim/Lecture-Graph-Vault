# Singular Values Identity 1.4.1b

**奇异值恒等式 1.4.1b** · identity · 1.4.1b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch01-S04-H01 - Definition Singular Values|Definition: Singular Values]] · [[Ch01-S04-H01 - Definition Singular Values|奇异值：定义]]

The pairing is computed in coordinates, which gives the identity we quote later:

$$
\langle x, y \rangle \;=\; \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i \overline{y_i}
 \;=\; \lVert P_{M} x \rVert^{2} + \lVert P_{M^{\perp}} x \rVert^{2}.
$$

恒等式在正交投影的讨论中反复使用。
