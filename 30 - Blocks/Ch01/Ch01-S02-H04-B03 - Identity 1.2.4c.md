# Norm Equivalence Identity 1.2.4c

**范数等价恒等式 1.2.4c** · identity · 1.2.4c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch01-S02-H04 - Key Lemma Norm Equivalence|Key Lemma: Norm Equivalence]] · [[Ch01-S02-H04 - Key Lemma Norm Equivalence|范数等价：关键引理]]

The pairing is computed in coordinates, which gives the identity we quote later:

$$
\langle x, y \rangle \;=\; \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i \overline{y_i}
 \;=\; \lVert P_{M} x \rVert^{2} + \lVert P_{M^{\perp}} x \rVert^{2}.
$$

恒等式在正交投影的讨论中反复使用。
