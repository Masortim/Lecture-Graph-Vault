# Hilbert Decomposition Example 1.3.2c

**希尔伯特分解例子 1.3.2c** · example · 1.3.2c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch01-S03-H02 - Notation Hilbert Decomposition|Notation: Hilbert Decomposition]] · [[Ch01-S03-H02 - Notation Hilbert Decomposition|希尔伯特分解：记号约定]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。
