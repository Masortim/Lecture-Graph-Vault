# Banach Limit Estimate 1.3.1c

**巴拿赫极限估计 1.3.1c** · estimate · 1.3.1c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch01-S03-H01 - Definition Banach Limit|Definition: Banach Limit]] · [[Ch01-S03-H01 - Definition Banach Limit|巴拿赫极限：定义]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。

Used together with: [[Ch04-S04-H04-B02 - Proposition 4.4.4b|Proposition 4.4.4b]], [[Ch06-S01-H03-B02 - Example 6.1.3b|Example 6.1.3b]].
