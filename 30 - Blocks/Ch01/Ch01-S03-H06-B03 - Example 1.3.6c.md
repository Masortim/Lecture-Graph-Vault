# Eigenvalue Bounds Example 1.3.6c

**特征值估计例子 1.3.6c** · example · 1.3.6c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch01-S03-H06 - Proof and Consequences Eigenvalue Bounds|Proof and Consequences: Eigenvalue Bounds]] · [[Ch01-S03-H06 - Proof and Consequences Eigenvalue Bounds|特征值估计：证明与推论]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。
