# Hilbert Decomposition Estimate 1.3.2b

**希尔伯特分解估计 1.3.2b** · estimate · 1.3.2b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch01-S03-H02 - Notation Hilbert Decomposition|Notation: Hilbert Decomposition]] · [[Ch01-S03-H02 - Notation Hilbert Decomposition|希尔伯特分解：记号约定]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。
