# Dual Space Example 1.2.1d

**对偶空间例子 1.2.1d** · example · 1.2.1d

⬆️ Part of [[Ch01-S02-H01 - Definition Dual Space|Definition: Dual Space]] · [[Ch01-S02-H01 - Definition Dual Space|对偶空间：定义]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。

Used together with: [[Ch03-S01-H05-B03 - Estimate 3.1.5c|Estimate 3.1.5c]], [[Ch04-S02-H04-B02 - Proposition 4.2.4b|Proposition 4.2.4b]].
