# Kernel and Range Proposition 1.3.5a

**核与值域命题 1.3.5a** · statement · 1.3.5a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch01-S03-H05 - Main Theorem Kernel and Range|Main Theorem: Kernel and Range]] · [[Ch01-S03-H05 - Main Theorem Kernel and Range|核与值域：主定理]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。
