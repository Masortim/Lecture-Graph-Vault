# Hahn-Banach Extension Proposition 8.3.3c

**哈恩-巴拿赫延拓命题 8.3.3c** · statement · 8.3.3c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch08-S03-H03 - Basic Properties Hahn-Banach Extension|Basic Properties: Hahn-Banach Extension]] · [[Ch08-S03-H03 - Basic Properties Hahn-Banach Extension|哈恩-巴拿赫延拓：基本性质]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。
