# Entropy Bound Example 8.4.3b

**熵界例子 8.4.3b** · example · 8.4.3b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch08-S04-H03 - Basic Properties Entropy Bound|Basic Properties: Entropy Bound]] · [[Ch08-S04-H03 - Basic Properties Entropy Bound|熵界：基本性质]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。
