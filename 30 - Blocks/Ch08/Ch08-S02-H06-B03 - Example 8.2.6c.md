# Trace Class Example 8.2.6c

**迹类例子 8.2.6c** · example · 8.2.6c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch08-S02-H06 - Proof and Consequences Trace Class|Proof and Consequences: Trace Class]] · [[Ch08-S02-H06 - Proof and Consequences Trace Class|迹类：证明与推论]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。
