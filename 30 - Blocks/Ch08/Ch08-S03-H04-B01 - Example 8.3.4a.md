# Open Mapping Example 8.3.4a

**开映射例子 8.3.4a** · example · 8.3.4a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch08-S03-H04 - Key Lemma Open Mapping|Key Lemma: Open Mapping]] · [[Ch08-S03-H04 - Key Lemma Open Mapping|开映射：关键引理]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。
