# Lipschitz Continuity Example 8.4.6c

**利普希茨连续性例子 8.4.6c** · example · 8.4.6c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch08-S04-H06 - Proof and Consequences Lipschitz Continuity|Proof and Consequences: Lipschitz Continuity]] · [[Ch08-S04-H06 - Proof and Consequences Lipschitz Continuity|利普希茨连续性：证明与推论]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。

Used together with: [[Ch03-S01-H05-B02 - Identity 3.1.5b|Identity 3.1.5b]], [[Ch04-S02-H04-B01 - Example 4.2.4a|Example 4.2.4a]].
