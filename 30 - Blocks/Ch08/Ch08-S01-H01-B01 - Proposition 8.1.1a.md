# Spectral Theorem Proposition 8.1.1a

**谱定理命题 8.1.1a** · statement · 8.1.1a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch08-S01-H01 - Definition Spectral Theorem|Definition: Spectral Theorem]] · [[Ch08-S01-H01 - Definition Spectral Theorem|谱定理：定义]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。
