# Fixed Point Proposition 8.1.4b

**不动点命题 8.1.4b** · statement · 8.1.4b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch08-S01-H04 - Key Lemma Fixed Point|Key Lemma: Fixed Point]] · [[Ch08-S01-H04 - Key Lemma Fixed Point|不动点：关键引理]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。

Used together with: [[Ch06-S01-H04-B03 - Identity 6.1.4c|Identity 6.1.4c]], [[Ch07-S02-H03-B03 - Proposition 7.2.3c|Proposition 7.2.3c]].
