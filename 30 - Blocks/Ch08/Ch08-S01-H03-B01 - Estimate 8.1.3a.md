# Contraction Mapping Estimate 8.1.3a

**压缩映射估计 8.1.3a** · estimate · 8.1.3a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch08-S01-H03 - Basic Properties Contraction Mapping|Basic Properties: Contraction Mapping]] · [[Ch08-S01-H03 - Basic Properties Contraction Mapping|压缩映射：基本性质]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。
