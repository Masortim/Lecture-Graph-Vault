# Closed Graph Proposition 8.3.5a

**闭图像命题 8.3.5a** · statement · 8.3.5a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch08-S03-H05 - Main Theorem Closed Graph|Main Theorem: Closed Graph]] · [[Ch08-S03-H05 - Main Theorem Closed Graph|闭图像：主定理]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。
