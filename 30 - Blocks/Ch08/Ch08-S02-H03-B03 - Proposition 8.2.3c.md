# Kernel and Range Proposition 8.2.3c

**核与值域命题 8.2.3c** · statement · 8.2.3c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch08-S02-H03 - Basic Properties Kernel and Range|Basic Properties: Kernel and Range]] · [[Ch08-S02-H03 - Basic Properties Kernel and Range|核与值域：基本性质]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。
