# Closed Graph Identity 8.3.5b

**闭图像恒等式 8.3.5b** · identity · 8.3.5b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch08-S03-H05 - Main Theorem Closed Graph|Main Theorem: Closed Graph]] · [[Ch08-S03-H05 - Main Theorem Closed Graph|闭图像：主定理]]

The pairing is computed in coordinates, which gives the identity we quote later:

$$
\langle x, y \rangle \;=\; \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i \overline{y_i}
 \;=\; \lVert P_{M} x \rVert^{2} + \lVert P_{M^{\perp}} x \rVert^{2}.
$$

恒等式在正交投影的讨论中反复使用。
