# Density Argument Example 8.2.2c

**稠密性论证例子 8.2.2c** · example · 8.2.2c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch08-S02-H02 - Notation Density Argument|Notation: Density Argument]] · [[Ch08-S02-H02 - Notation Density Argument|稠密性论证：记号约定]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。

Used together with: [[Ch07-S02-H05-B03 - Estimate 7.2.5c|Estimate 7.2.5c]], [[Ch08-S03-H04-B02 - Proposition 8.3.4b|Proposition 8.3.4b]].
