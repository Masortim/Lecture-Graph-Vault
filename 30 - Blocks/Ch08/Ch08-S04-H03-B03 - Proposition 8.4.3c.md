# Entropy Bound Proposition 8.4.3c

**熵界命题 8.4.3c** · statement · 8.4.3c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch08-S04-H03 - Basic Properties Entropy Bound|Basic Properties: Entropy Bound]] · [[Ch08-S04-H03 - Basic Properties Entropy Bound|熵界：基本性质]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。
