# Uniform Boundedness Example 8.3.6c

**一致有界性例子 8.3.6c** · example · 8.3.6c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch08-S03-H06 - Proof and Consequences Uniform Boundedness|Proof and Consequences: Uniform Boundedness]] · [[Ch08-S03-H06 - Proof and Consequences Uniform Boundedness|一致有界性：证明与推论]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。

Used together with: [[Ch01-S02-H04-B02 - Proposition 1.2.4b|Proposition 1.2.4b]], [[Ch02-S03-H03-B02 - Example 2.3.3b|Example 2.3.3b]].
