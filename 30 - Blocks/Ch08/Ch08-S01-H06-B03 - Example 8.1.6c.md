# Hilbert Decomposition Example 8.1.6c

**希尔伯特分解例子 8.1.6c** · example · 8.1.6c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch08-S01-H06 - Proof and Consequences Hilbert Decomposition|Proof and Consequences: Hilbert Decomposition]] · [[Ch08-S01-H06 - Proof and Consequences Hilbert Decomposition|希尔伯特分解：证明与推论]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。

Used together with: [[Ch06-S04-H02-B03 - Example 6.4.2c|Example 6.4.2c]], [[Ch08-S01-H01-B03 - Estimate 8.1.1c|Estimate 8.1.1c]].
