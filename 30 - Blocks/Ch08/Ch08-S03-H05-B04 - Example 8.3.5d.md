# Closed Graph Example 8.3.5d

**闭图像例子 8.3.5d** · example · 8.3.5d

⬆️ Part of [[Ch08-S03-H05 - Main Theorem Closed Graph|Main Theorem: Closed Graph]] · [[Ch08-S03-H05 - Main Theorem Closed Graph|闭图像：主定理]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。

Used together with: [[Ch01-S01-H04-B02 - Proposition 1.1.4b|Proposition 1.1.4b]], [[Ch02-S02-H03-B02 - Example 2.2.3b|Example 2.2.3b]].
