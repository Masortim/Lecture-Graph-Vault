# Concentration Inequality Proposition 8.4.4b

**集中不等式命题 8.4.4b** · statement · 8.4.4b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch08-S04-H04 - Key Lemma Concentration Inequality|Key Lemma: Concentration Inequality]] · [[Ch08-S04-H04 - Key Lemma Concentration Inequality|集中不等式：关键引理]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。
