# Spectral Theorem Identity 8.1.1b

**谱定理恒等式 8.1.1b** · identity · 8.1.1b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch08-S01-H01 - Definition Spectral Theorem|Definition: Spectral Theorem]] · [[Ch08-S01-H01 - Definition Spectral Theorem|谱定理：定义]]

The pairing is computed in coordinates, which gives the identity we quote later:

$$
\langle x, y \rangle \;=\; \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i \overline{y_i}
 \;=\; \lVert P_{M} x \rVert^{2} + \lVert P_{M^{\perp}} x \rVert^{2}.
$$

恒等式在正交投影的讨论中反复使用。
