# Spectral Gap Estimate 5.4.5c

**谱隙估计 5.4.5c** · estimate · 5.4.5c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch05-S04-H05 - Main Theorem Spectral Gap|Main Theorem: Spectral Gap]] · [[Ch05-S04-H05 - Main Theorem Spectral Gap|谱隙：主定理]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。

Used together with: [[Ch08-S04-H03-B02 - Example 8.4.3b|Example 8.4.3b]], [[Ch01-S01-H02-B01 - Identity 1.1.2a|Identity 1.1.2a]].
