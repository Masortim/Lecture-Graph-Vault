# Feature Map Estimate 5.4.1c

**特征映射估计 5.4.1c** · estimate · 5.4.1c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch05-S04-H01 - Definition Feature Map|Definition: Feature Map]] · [[Ch05-S04-H01 - Definition Feature Map|特征映射：定义]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。

Used together with: [[Ch07-S03-H05-B02 - Identity 7.3.5b|Identity 7.3.5b]], [[Ch08-S04-H04-B01 - Example 8.4.4a|Example 8.4.4a]].
