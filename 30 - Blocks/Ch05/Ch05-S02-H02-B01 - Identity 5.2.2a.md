# Open Mapping Identity 5.2.2a

**开映射恒等式 5.2.2a** · identity · 5.2.2a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch05-S02-H02 - Notation Open Mapping|Notation: Open Mapping]] · [[Ch05-S02-H02 - Notation Open Mapping|开映射：记号约定]]

The pairing is computed in coordinates, which gives the identity we quote later:

$$
\langle x, y \rangle \;=\; \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i \overline{y_i}
 \;=\; \lVert P_{M} x \rVert^{2} + \lVert P_{M^{\perp}} x \rVert^{2}.
$$

恒等式在正交投影的讨论中反复使用。
