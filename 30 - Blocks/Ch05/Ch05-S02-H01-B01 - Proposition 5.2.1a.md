# Hahn-Banach Extension Proposition 5.2.1a

**哈恩-巴拿赫延拓命题 5.2.1a** · statement · 5.2.1a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch05-S02-H01 - Definition Hahn-Banach Extension|Definition: Hahn-Banach Extension]] · [[Ch05-S02-H01 - Definition Hahn-Banach Extension|哈恩-巴拿赫延拓：定义]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。
