# Uniform Boundedness Identity 5.2.4c

**一致有界性恒等式 5.2.4c** · identity · 5.2.4c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch05-S02-H04 - Key Lemma Uniform Boundedness|Key Lemma: Uniform Boundedness]] · [[Ch05-S02-H04 - Key Lemma Uniform Boundedness|一致有界性：关键引理]]

The pairing is computed in coordinates, which gives the identity we quote later:

$$
\langle x, y \rangle \;=\; \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i \overline{y_i}
 \;=\; \lVert P_{M} x \rVert^{2} + \lVert P_{M^{\perp}} x \rVert^{2}.
$$
