# Open Mapping Example 5.2.2c

**开映射例子 5.2.2c** · example · 5.2.2c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch05-S02-H02 - Notation Open Mapping|Notation: Open Mapping]] · [[Ch05-S02-H02 - Notation Open Mapping|开映射：记号约定]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。
