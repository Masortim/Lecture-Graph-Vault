# Radon-Nikodym Derivative Example 5.2.6c

**拉东-尼科迪姆导数例子 5.2.6c** · example · 5.2.6c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch05-S02-H06 - Proof and Consequences Radon-Nikodym Derivative|Proof and Consequences: Radon-Nikodym Derivative]] · [[Ch05-S02-H06 - Proof and Consequences Radon-Nikodym Derivative|拉东-尼科迪姆导数：证明与推论]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。
