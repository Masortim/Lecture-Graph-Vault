# Hahn-Banach Extension Estimate 5.2.1c

**哈恩-巴拿赫延拓估计 5.2.1c** · estimate · 5.2.1c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch05-S02-H01 - Definition Hahn-Banach Extension|Definition: Hahn-Banach Extension]] · [[Ch05-S02-H01 - Definition Hahn-Banach Extension|哈恩-巴拿赫延拓：定义]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。

Used together with: [[Ch04-S01-H03-B03 - Proposition 4.1.3c|Proposition 4.1.3c]], [[Ch05-S02-H02-B02 - Estimate 5.2.2b|Estimate 5.2.2b]].
