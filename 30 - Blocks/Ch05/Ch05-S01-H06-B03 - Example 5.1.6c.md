# Separation Theorem Example 5.1.6c

**分离定理例子 5.1.6c** · example · 5.1.6c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch05-S01-H06 - Proof and Consequences Separation Theorem|Proof and Consequences: Separation Theorem]] · [[Ch05-S01-H06 - Proof and Consequences Separation Theorem|分离定理：证明与推论]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。

Used together with: [[Ch03-S04-H02-B03 - Example 3.4.2c|Example 3.4.2c]], [[Ch05-S01-H01-B03 - Estimate 5.1.1c|Estimate 5.1.1c]].
