# Kernel Matrix Estimate 5.4.2b

**核矩阵估计 5.4.2b** · estimate · 5.4.2b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch05-S04-H02 - Notation Kernel Matrix|Notation: Kernel Matrix]] · [[Ch05-S04-H02 - Notation Kernel Matrix|核矩阵：记号约定]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。

Used together with: [[Ch07-S04-H05-B02 - Identity 7.4.5b|Identity 7.4.5b]], [[Ch09-S01-H04-B01 - Example 9.1.4a|Example 9.1.4a]].
