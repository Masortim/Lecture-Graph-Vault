# Trace Class Proposition 5.1.4b

**迹类命题 5.1.4b** · statement · 5.1.4b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch05-S01-H04 - Key Lemma Trace Class|Key Lemma: Trace Class]] · [[Ch05-S01-H04 - Key Lemma Trace Class|迹类：关键引理]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。

Used together with: [[Ch03-S01-H04-B03 - Identity 3.1.4c|Identity 3.1.4c]], [[Ch04-S02-H03-B03 - Proposition 4.2.3c|Proposition 4.2.3c]].
