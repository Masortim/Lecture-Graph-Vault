# Duality Gap Example 5.3.5d

**对偶间隙例子 5.3.5d** · example · 5.3.5d

⬆️ Part of [[Ch05-S03-H05 - Main Theorem Duality Gap|Main Theorem: Duality Gap]] · [[Ch05-S03-H05 - Main Theorem Duality Gap|对偶间隙：主定理]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。
