# Concentration Inequality Estimate 5.3.2b

**集中不等式估计 5.3.2b** · estimate · 5.3.2b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch05-S03-H02 - Notation Concentration Inequality|Notation: Concentration Inequality]] · [[Ch05-S03-H02 - Notation Concentration Inequality|集中不等式：记号约定]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。

Used together with: [[Ch06-S01-H04-B02 - Proposition 6.1.4b|Proposition 6.1.4b]], [[Ch07-S02-H03-B02 - Example 7.2.3b|Example 7.2.3b]].
