# Generalization Bound Identity 5.4.4c

**泛化界恒等式 5.4.4c** · identity · 5.4.4c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch05-S04-H04 - Key Lemma Generalization Bound|Key Lemma: Generalization Bound]] · [[Ch05-S04-H04 - Key Lemma Generalization Bound|泛化界：关键引理]]

The pairing is computed in coordinates, which gives the identity we quote later:

$$
\langle x, y \rangle \;=\; \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i \overline{y_i}
 \;=\; \lVert P_{M} x \rVert^{2} + \lVert P_{M^{\perp}} x \rVert^{2}.
$$

恒等式在正交投影的讨论中反复使用。
