# Entropy Bound Proposition 5.3.1a

**熵界命题 5.3.1a** · statement · 5.3.1a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch05-S03-H01 - Definition Entropy Bound|Definition: Entropy Bound]] · [[Ch05-S03-H01 - Definition Entropy Bound|熵界：定义]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。

Used together with: [[Ch05-S03-H06-B02 - Estimate 5.3.6b|Estimate 5.3.6b]], [[Ch06-S04-H05-B02 - Identity 6.4.5b|Identity 6.4.5b]].
