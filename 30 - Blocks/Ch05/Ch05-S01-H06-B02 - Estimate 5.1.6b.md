# Separation Theorem Estimate 5.1.6b

**分离定理估计 5.1.6b** · estimate · 5.1.6b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch05-S01-H06 - Proof and Consequences Separation Theorem|Proof and Consequences: Separation Theorem]] · [[Ch05-S01-H06 - Proof and Consequences Separation Theorem|分离定理：证明与推论]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。
