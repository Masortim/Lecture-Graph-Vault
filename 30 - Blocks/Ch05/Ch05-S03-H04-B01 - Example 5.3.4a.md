# Lipschitz Continuity Example 5.3.4a

**利普希茨连续性例子 5.3.4a** · example · 5.3.4a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch05-S03-H04 - Key Lemma Lipschitz Continuity|Key Lemma: Lipschitz Continuity]] · [[Ch05-S03-H04 - Key Lemma Lipschitz Continuity|利普希茨连续性：关键引理]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。
