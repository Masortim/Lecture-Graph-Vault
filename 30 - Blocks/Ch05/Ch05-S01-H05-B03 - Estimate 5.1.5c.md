# Convex Hull Estimate 5.1.5c

**凸包估计 5.1.5c** · estimate · 5.1.5c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch05-S01-H05 - Main Theorem Convex Hull|Main Theorem: Convex Hull]] · [[Ch05-S01-H05 - Main Theorem Convex Hull|凸包：主定理]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。
