# Kernel and Range Estimate 5.1.1c

**核与值域估计 5.1.1c** · estimate · 5.1.1c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch05-S01-H01 - Definition Kernel and Range|Definition: Kernel and Range]] · [[Ch05-S01-H01 - Definition Kernel and Range|核与值域：定义]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。
