# Eigenvalue Bounds Example 5.1.2c

**特征值估计例子 5.1.2c** · example · 5.1.2c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch05-S01-H02 - Notation Eigenvalue Bounds|Notation: Eigenvalue Bounds]] · [[Ch05-S01-H02 - Notation Eigenvalue Bounds|特征值估计：记号约定]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。
