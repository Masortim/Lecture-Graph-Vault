# Sample Complexity Estimate 5.4.3a

**样本复杂度估计 5.4.3a** · estimate · 5.4.3a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch05-S04-H03 - Basic Properties Sample Complexity|Basic Properties: Sample Complexity]] · [[Ch05-S04-H03 - Basic Properties Sample Complexity|样本复杂度：基本性质]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。
