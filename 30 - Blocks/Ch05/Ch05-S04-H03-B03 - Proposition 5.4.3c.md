# Sample Complexity Proposition 5.4.3c

**样本复杂度命题 5.4.3c** · statement · 5.4.3c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch05-S04-H03 - Basic Properties Sample Complexity|Basic Properties: Sample Complexity]] · [[Ch05-S04-H03 - Basic Properties Sample Complexity|样本复杂度：基本性质]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。
