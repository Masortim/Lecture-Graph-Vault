# Kernel Matrix Identity 5.4.2a

**核矩阵恒等式 5.4.2a** · identity · 5.4.2a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch05-S04-H02 - Notation Kernel Matrix|Notation: Kernel Matrix]] · [[Ch05-S04-H02 - Notation Kernel Matrix|核矩阵：记号约定]]

The pairing is computed in coordinates, which gives the identity we quote later:

$$
\langle x, y \rangle \;=\; \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i \overline{y_i}
 \;=\; \lVert P_{M} x \rVert^{2} + \lVert P_{M^{\perp}} x \rVert^{2}.
$$

恒等式在正交投影的讨论中反复使用。
