# Generalization Bound Example 5.4.4a

**泛化界例子 5.4.4a** · example · 5.4.4a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch05-S04-H04 - Key Lemma Generalization Bound|Key Lemma: Generalization Bound]] · [[Ch05-S04-H04 - Key Lemma Generalization Bound|泛化界：关键引理]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。
