# Regularization Example 5.3.6c

**正则化例子 5.3.6c** · example · 5.3.6c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch05-S03-H06 - Proof and Consequences Regularization|Proof and Consequences: Regularization]] · [[Ch05-S03-H06 - Proof and Consequences Regularization|正则化：证明与推论]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。
