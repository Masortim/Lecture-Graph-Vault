# Radon-Nikodym Derivative Estimate 5.2.6b

**拉东-尼科迪姆导数估计 5.2.6b** · estimate · 5.2.6b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch05-S02-H06 - Proof and Consequences Radon-Nikodym Derivative|Proof and Consequences: Radon-Nikodym Derivative]] · [[Ch05-S02-H06 - Proof and Consequences Radon-Nikodym Derivative|拉东-尼科迪姆导数：证明与推论]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。
