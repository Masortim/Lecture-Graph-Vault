# Feature Map Identity 5.4.1b

**特征映射恒等式 5.4.1b** · identity · 5.4.1b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch05-S04-H01 - Definition Feature Map|Definition: Feature Map]] · [[Ch05-S04-H01 - Definition Feature Map|特征映射：定义]]

The pairing is computed in coordinates, which gives the identity we quote later:

$$
\langle x, y \rangle \;=\; \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i \overline{y_i}
 \;=\; \lVert P_{M} x \rVert^{2} + \lVert P_{M^{\perp}} x \rVert^{2}.
$$

恒等式在正交投影的讨论中反复使用。
