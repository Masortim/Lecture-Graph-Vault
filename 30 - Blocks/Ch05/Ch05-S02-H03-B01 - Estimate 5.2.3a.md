# Closed Graph Estimate 5.2.3a

**闭图像估计 5.2.3a** · estimate · 5.2.3a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch05-S02-H03 - Basic Properties Closed Graph|Basic Properties: Closed Graph]] · [[Ch05-S02-H03 - Basic Properties Closed Graph|闭图像：基本性质]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。
