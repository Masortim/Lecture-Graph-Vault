# Trace Class Example 6.4.2c

**迹类例子 6.4.2c** · example · 6.4.2c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch06-S04-H02 - Notation Trace Class|Notation: Trace Class]] · [[Ch06-S04-H02 - Notation Trace Class|迹类：记号约定]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。

Used together with: [[Ch06-S01-H01-B02 - Identity 6.1.1b|Identity 6.1.1b]], [[Ch07-S01-H06-B01 - Identity 7.1.6a|Identity 7.1.6a]].
