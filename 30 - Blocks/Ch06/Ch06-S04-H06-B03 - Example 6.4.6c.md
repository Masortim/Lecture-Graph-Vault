# Open Mapping Example 6.4.6c

**开映射例子 6.4.6c** · example · 6.4.6c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch06-S04-H06 - Proof and Consequences Open Mapping|Proof and Consequences: Open Mapping]] · [[Ch06-S04-H06 - Proof and Consequences Open Mapping|开映射：证明与推论]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。
