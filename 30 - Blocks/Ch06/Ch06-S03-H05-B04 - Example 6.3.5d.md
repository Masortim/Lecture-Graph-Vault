# Kernel and Range Example 6.3.5d

**核与值域例子 6.3.5d** · example · 6.3.5d

⬆️ Part of [[Ch06-S03-H05 - Main Theorem Kernel and Range|Main Theorem: Kernel and Range]] · [[Ch06-S03-H05 - Main Theorem Kernel and Range|核与值域：主定理]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。
