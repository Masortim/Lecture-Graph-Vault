# Adjoint Operator Estimate 6.2.2b

**伴随算子估计 6.2.2b** · estimate · 6.2.2b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch06-S02-H02 - Notation Adjoint Operator|Notation: Adjoint Operator]] · [[Ch06-S02-H02 - Notation Adjoint Operator|伴随算子：记号约定]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。
