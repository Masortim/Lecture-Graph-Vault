# Trace Class Identity 6.4.2a

**迹类恒等式 6.4.2a** · identity · 6.4.2a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch06-S04-H02 - Notation Trace Class|Notation: Trace Class]] · [[Ch06-S04-H02 - Notation Trace Class|迹类：记号约定]]

The pairing is computed in coordinates, which gives the identity we quote later:

$$
\langle x, y \rangle \;=\; \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i \overline{y_i}
 \;=\; \lVert P_{M} x \rVert^{2} + \lVert P_{M^{\perp}} x \rVert^{2}.
$$

恒等式在正交投影的讨论中反复使用。
