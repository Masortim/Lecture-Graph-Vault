# Norm Equivalence Proposition 6.2.4b

**范数等价命题 6.2.4b** · statement · 6.2.4b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch06-S02-H04 - Key Lemma Norm Equivalence|Key Lemma: Norm Equivalence]] · [[Ch06-S02-H04 - Key Lemma Norm Equivalence|范数等价：关键引理]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。
