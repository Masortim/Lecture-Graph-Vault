# Contraction Mapping Proposition 6.2.5a

**压缩映射命题 6.2.5a** · statement · 6.2.5a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch06-S02-H05 - Main Theorem Contraction Mapping|Main Theorem: Contraction Mapping]] · [[Ch06-S02-H05 - Main Theorem Contraction Mapping|压缩映射：主定理]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。
