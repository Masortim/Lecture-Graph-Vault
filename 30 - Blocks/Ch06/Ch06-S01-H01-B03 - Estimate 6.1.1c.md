# Vector Space Estimate 6.1.1c

**向量空间估计 6.1.1c** · estimate · 6.1.1c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch06-S01-H01 - Definition Vector Space|Definition: Vector Space]] · [[Ch06-S01-H01 - Definition Vector Space|向量空间：定义]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。
