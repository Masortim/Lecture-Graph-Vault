# Separation Theorem Proposition 6.4.4b

**分离定理命题 6.4.4b** · statement · 6.4.4b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch06-S04-H04 - Key Lemma Separation Theorem|Key Lemma: Separation Theorem]] · [[Ch06-S04-H04 - Key Lemma Separation Theorem|分离定理：关键引理]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。
