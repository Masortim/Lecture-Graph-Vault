# Weak Convergence Proposition 6.3.3c

**弱收敛命题 6.3.3c** · statement · 6.3.3c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch06-S03-H03 - Basic Properties Weak Convergence|Basic Properties: Weak Convergence]] · [[Ch06-S03-H03 - Basic Properties Weak Convergence|弱收敛：基本性质]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。
