# Density Argument Identity 6.3.4c

**稠密性论证恒等式 6.3.4c** · identity · 6.3.4c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch06-S03-H04 - Key Lemma Density Argument|Key Lemma: Density Argument]] · [[Ch06-S03-H04 - Key Lemma Density Argument|稠密性论证：关键引理]]

The pairing is computed in coordinates, which gives the identity we quote later:

$$
\langle x, y \rangle \;=\; \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i \overline{y_i}
 \;=\; \lVert P_{M} x \rVert^{2} + \lVert P_{M^{\perp}} x \rVert^{2}.
$$
