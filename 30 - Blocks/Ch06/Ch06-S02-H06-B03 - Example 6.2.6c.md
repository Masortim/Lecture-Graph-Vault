# Fixed Point Example 6.2.6c

**不动点例子 6.2.6c** · example · 6.2.6c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch06-S02-H06 - Proof and Consequences Fixed Point|Proof and Consequences: Fixed Point]] · [[Ch06-S02-H06 - Proof and Consequences Fixed Point|不动点：证明与推论]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。
