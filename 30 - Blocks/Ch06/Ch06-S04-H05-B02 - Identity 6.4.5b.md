# Hahn-Banach Extension Identity 6.4.5b

**哈恩-巴拿赫延拓恒等式 6.4.5b** · identity · 6.4.5b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch06-S04-H05 - Main Theorem Hahn-Banach Extension|Main Theorem: Hahn-Banach Extension]] · [[Ch06-S04-H05 - Main Theorem Hahn-Banach Extension|哈恩-巴拿赫延拓：主定理]]

The pairing is computed in coordinates, which gives the identity we quote later:

$$
\langle x, y \rangle \;=\; \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i \overline{y_i}
 \;=\; \lVert P_{M} x \rVert^{2} + \lVert P_{M^{\perp}} x \rVert^{2}.
$$

恒等式在正交投影的讨论中反复使用。
