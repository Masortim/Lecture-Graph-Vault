# Open Mapping Estimate 6.4.6b

**开映射估计 6.4.6b** · estimate · 6.4.6b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch06-S04-H06 - Proof and Consequences Open Mapping|Proof and Consequences: Open Mapping]] · [[Ch06-S04-H06 - Proof and Consequences Open Mapping|开映射：证明与推论]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。
