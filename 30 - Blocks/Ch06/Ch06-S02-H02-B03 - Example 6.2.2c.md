# Adjoint Operator Example 6.2.2c

**伴随算子例子 6.2.2c** · example · 6.2.2c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch06-S02-H02 - Notation Adjoint Operator|Notation: Adjoint Operator]] · [[Ch06-S02-H02 - Notation Adjoint Operator|伴随算子：记号约定]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。
