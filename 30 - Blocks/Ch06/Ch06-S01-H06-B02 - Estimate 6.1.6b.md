# Basis and Coordinates Estimate 6.1.6b

**基与坐标估计 6.1.6b** · estimate · 6.1.6b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch06-S01-H06 - Proof and Consequences Basis and Coordinates|Proof and Consequences: Basis and Coordinates]] · [[Ch06-S01-H06 - Proof and Consequences Basis and Coordinates|基与坐标：证明与推论]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。

Used together with: [[Ch01-S03-H06-B03 - Example 1.3.6c|Example 1.3.6c]], [[Ch02-S04-H05-B03 - Estimate 2.4.5c|Estimate 2.4.5c]].
