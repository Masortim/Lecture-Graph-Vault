# Spectral Theorem Estimate 9.3.5c

**谱定理估计 9.3.5c** · estimate · 9.3.5c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch09-S03-H05 - Main Theorem Spectral Theorem|Main Theorem: Spectral Theorem]] · [[Ch09-S03-H05 - Main Theorem Spectral Theorem|谱定理：主定理]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。

Used together with: [[Ch08-S01-H02-B02 - Estimate 8.1.2b|Estimate 8.1.2b]], [[Ch09-S02-H01-B02 - Identity 9.2.1b|Identity 9.2.1b]].
