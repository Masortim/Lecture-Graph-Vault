# Metric Completion Identity 9.2.6a

**度量完备化恒等式 9.2.6a** · identity · 9.2.6a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch09-S02-H06 - Proof and Consequences Metric Completion|Proof and Consequences: Metric Completion]] · [[Ch09-S02-H06 - Proof and Consequences Metric Completion|度量完备化：证明与推论]]

The pairing is computed in coordinates, which gives the identity we quote later:

$$
\langle x, y \rangle \;=\; \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i \overline{y_i}
 \;=\; \lVert P_{M} x \rVert^{2} + \lVert P_{M^{\perp}} x \rVert^{2}.
$$

恒等式在正交投影的讨论中反复使用。
