# Orthogonal Projection Proposition 9.3.1a

**正交投影命题 9.3.1a** · statement · 9.3.1a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch09-S03-H01 - Definition Orthogonal Projection|Definition: Orthogonal Projection]] · [[Ch09-S03-H01 - Definition Orthogonal Projection|正交投影：定义]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。
