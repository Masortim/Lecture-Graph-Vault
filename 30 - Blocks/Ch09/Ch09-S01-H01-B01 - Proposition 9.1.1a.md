# Duality Gap Proposition 9.1.1a

**对偶间隙命题 9.1.1a** · statement · 9.1.1a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch09-S01-H01 - Definition Duality Gap|Definition: Duality Gap]] · [[Ch09-S01-H01 - Definition Duality Gap|对偶间隙：定义]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。
