# Fixed Point Example 9.4.2c

**不动点例子 9.4.2c** · example · 9.4.2c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch09-S04-H02 - Notation Fixed Point|Notation: Fixed Point]] · [[Ch09-S04-H02 - Notation Fixed Point|不动点：记号约定]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。
