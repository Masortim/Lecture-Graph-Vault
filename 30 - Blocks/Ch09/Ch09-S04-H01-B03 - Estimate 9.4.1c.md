# Contraction Mapping Estimate 9.4.1c

**压缩映射估计 9.4.1c** · estimate · 9.4.1c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch09-S04-H01 - Definition Contraction Mapping|Definition: Contraction Mapping]] · [[Ch09-S04-H01 - Definition Contraction Mapping|压缩映射：定义]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。

Used together with: [[Ch08-S03-H05-B02 - Identity 8.3.5b|Identity 8.3.5b]], [[Ch09-S04-H04-B01 - Example 9.4.4a|Example 9.4.4a]].
