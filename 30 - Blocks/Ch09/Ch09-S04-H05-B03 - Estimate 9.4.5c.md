# Weak Convergence Estimate 9.4.5c

**弱收敛估计 9.4.5c** · estimate · 9.4.5c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch09-S04-H05 - Main Theorem Weak Convergence|Main Theorem: Weak Convergence]] · [[Ch09-S04-H05 - Main Theorem Weak Convergence|弱收敛：主定理]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。
