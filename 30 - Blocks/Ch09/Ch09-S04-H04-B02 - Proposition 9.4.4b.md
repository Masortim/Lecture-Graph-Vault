# Hilbert Decomposition Proposition 9.4.4b

**希尔伯特分解命题 9.4.4b** · statement · 9.4.4b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch09-S04-H04 - Key Lemma Hilbert Decomposition|Key Lemma: Hilbert Decomposition]] · [[Ch09-S04-H04 - Key Lemma Hilbert Decomposition|希尔伯特分解：关键引理]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。
