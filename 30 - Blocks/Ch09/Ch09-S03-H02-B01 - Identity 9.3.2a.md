# Basis and Coordinates Identity 9.3.2a

**基与坐标恒等式 9.3.2a** · identity · 9.3.2a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch09-S03-H02 - Notation Basis and Coordinates|Notation: Basis and Coordinates]] · [[Ch09-S03-H02 - Notation Basis and Coordinates|基与坐标：记号约定]]

The pairing is computed in coordinates, which gives the identity we quote later:

$$
\langle x, y \rangle \;=\; \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i \overline{y_i}
 \;=\; \lVert P_{M} x \rVert^{2} + \lVert P_{M^{\perp}} x \rVert^{2}.
$$

恒等式在正交投影的讨论中反复使用。
