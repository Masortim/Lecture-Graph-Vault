# Banach Limit Example 9.4.3b

**巴拿赫极限例子 9.4.3b** · example · 9.4.3b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch09-S04-H03 - Basic Properties Banach Limit|Basic Properties: Banach Limit]] · [[Ch09-S04-H03 - Basic Properties Banach Limit|巴拿赫极限：基本性质]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。
