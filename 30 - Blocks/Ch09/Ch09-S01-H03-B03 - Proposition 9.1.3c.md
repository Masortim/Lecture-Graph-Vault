# Feature Map Proposition 9.1.3c

**特征映射命题 9.1.3c** · statement · 9.1.3c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch09-S01-H03 - Basic Properties Feature Map|Basic Properties: Feature Map]] · [[Ch09-S01-H03 - Basic Properties Feature Map|特征映射：基本性质]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。
