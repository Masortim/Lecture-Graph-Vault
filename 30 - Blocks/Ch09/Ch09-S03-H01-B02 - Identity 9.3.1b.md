# Orthogonal Projection Identity 9.3.1b

**正交投影恒等式 9.3.1b** · identity · 9.3.1b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch09-S03-H01 - Definition Orthogonal Projection|Definition: Orthogonal Projection]] · [[Ch09-S03-H01 - Definition Orthogonal Projection|正交投影：定义]]

The pairing is computed in coordinates, which gives the identity we quote later:

$$
\langle x, y \rangle \;=\; \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i \overline{y_i}
 \;=\; \lVert P_{M} x \rVert^{2} + \lVert P_{M^{\perp}} x \rVert^{2}.
$$

恒等式在正交投影的讨论中反复使用。
