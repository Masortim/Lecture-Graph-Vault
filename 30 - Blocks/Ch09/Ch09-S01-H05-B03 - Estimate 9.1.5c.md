# Sample Complexity Estimate 9.1.5c

**样本复杂度估计 9.1.5c** · estimate · 9.1.5c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch09-S01-H05 - Main Theorem Sample Complexity|Main Theorem: Sample Complexity]] · [[Ch09-S01-H05 - Main Theorem Sample Complexity|样本复杂度：主定理]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。

Used together with: [[Ch04-S02-H06-B03 - Example 4.2.6c|Example 4.2.6c]], [[Ch05-S03-H05-B03 - Estimate 5.3.5c|Estimate 5.3.5c]].
