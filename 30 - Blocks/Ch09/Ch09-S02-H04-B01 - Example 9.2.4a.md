# Linear Operator Example 9.2.4a

**线性算子例子 9.2.4a** · example · 9.2.4a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch09-S02-H04 - Key Lemma Linear Operator|Key Lemma: Linear Operator]] · [[Ch09-S02-H04 - Key Lemma Linear Operator|线性算子：关键引理]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。

Used together with: [[Ch05-S04-H03-B03 - Proposition 5.4.3c|Proposition 5.4.3c]], [[Ch07-S01-H02-B02 - Estimate 7.1.2b|Estimate 7.1.2b]].
