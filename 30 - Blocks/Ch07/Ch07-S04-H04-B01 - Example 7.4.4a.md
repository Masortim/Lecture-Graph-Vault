# Basis and Coordinates Example 7.4.4a

**基与坐标例子 7.4.4a** · example · 7.4.4a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch07-S04-H04 - Key Lemma Basis and Coordinates|Key Lemma: Basis and Coordinates]] · [[Ch07-S04-H04 - Key Lemma Basis and Coordinates|基与坐标：关键引理]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。
