# Uniform Boundedness Example 7.1.2c

**一致有界性例子 7.1.2c** · example · 7.1.2c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch07-S01-H02 - Notation Uniform Boundedness|Notation: Uniform Boundedness]] · [[Ch07-S01-H02 - Notation Uniform Boundedness|一致有界性：记号约定]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。
