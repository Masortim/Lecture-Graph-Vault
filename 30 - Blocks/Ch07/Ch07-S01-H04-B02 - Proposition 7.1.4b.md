# Radon-Nikodym Derivative Proposition 7.1.4b

**拉东-尼科迪姆导数命题 7.1.4b** · statement · 7.1.4b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch07-S01-H04 - Key Lemma Radon-Nikodym Derivative|Key Lemma: Radon-Nikodym Derivative]] · [[Ch07-S01-H04 - Key Lemma Radon-Nikodym Derivative|拉东-尼科迪姆导数：关键引理]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。
