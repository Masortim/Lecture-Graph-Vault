# Sample Complexity Example 7.3.1d

**样本复杂度例子 7.3.1d** · example · 7.3.1d

⬆️ Part of [[Ch07-S03-H01 - Definition Sample Complexity|Definition: Sample Complexity]] · [[Ch07-S03-H01 - Definition Sample Complexity|样本复杂度：定义]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。

Used together with: [[Ch01-S04-H06-B02 - Estimate 1.4.6b|Estimate 1.4.6b]], [[Ch03-S01-H05-B02 - Identity 3.1.5b|Identity 3.1.5b]].
