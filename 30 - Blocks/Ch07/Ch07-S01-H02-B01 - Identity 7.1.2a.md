# Uniform Boundedness Identity 7.1.2a

**一致有界性恒等式 7.1.2a** · identity · 7.1.2a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch07-S01-H02 - Notation Uniform Boundedness|Notation: Uniform Boundedness]] · [[Ch07-S01-H02 - Notation Uniform Boundedness|一致有界性：记号约定]]

The pairing is computed in coordinates, which gives the identity we quote later:

$$
\langle x, y \rangle \;=\; \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i \overline{y_i}
 \;=\; \lVert P_{M} x \rVert^{2} + \lVert P_{M^{\perp}} x \rVert^{2}.
$$
