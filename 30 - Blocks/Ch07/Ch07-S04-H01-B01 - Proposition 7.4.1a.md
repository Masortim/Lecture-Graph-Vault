# Compact Set Proposition 7.4.1a

**紧集命题 7.4.1a** · statement · 7.4.1a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch07-S04-H01 - Definition Compact Set|Definition: Compact Set]] · [[Ch07-S04-H01 - Definition Compact Set|紧集：定义]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。

Used together with: [[Ch03-S03-H01-B02 - Identity 3.3.1b|Identity 3.3.1b]], [[Ch04-S03-H06-B01 - Identity 4.3.6a|Identity 4.3.6a]].
