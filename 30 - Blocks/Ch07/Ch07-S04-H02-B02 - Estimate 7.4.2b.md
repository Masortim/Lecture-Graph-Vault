# Metric Completion Estimate 7.4.2b

**度量完备化估计 7.4.2b** · estimate · 7.4.2b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch07-S04-H02 - Notation Metric Completion|Notation: Metric Completion]] · [[Ch07-S04-H02 - Notation Metric Completion|度量完备化：记号约定]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。
