# Radon-Nikodym Derivative Example 7.1.4a

**拉东-尼科迪姆导数例子 7.1.4a** · example · 7.1.4a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch07-S01-H04 - Key Lemma Radon-Nikodym Derivative|Key Lemma: Radon-Nikodym Derivative]] · [[Ch07-S01-H04 - Key Lemma Radon-Nikodym Derivative|拉东-尼科迪姆导数：关键引理]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。

Used together with: [[Ch08-S01-H02-B03 - Example 8.1.2c|Example 8.1.2c]], [[Ch09-S02-H01-B03 - Estimate 9.2.1c|Estimate 9.2.1c]].
