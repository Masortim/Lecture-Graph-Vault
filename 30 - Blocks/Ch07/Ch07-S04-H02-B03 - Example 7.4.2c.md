# Metric Completion Example 7.4.2c

**度量完备化例子 7.4.2c** · example · 7.4.2c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch07-S04-H02 - Notation Metric Completion|Notation: Metric Completion]] · [[Ch07-S04-H02 - Notation Metric Completion|度量完备化：记号约定]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。

Used together with: [[Ch04-S01-H01-B02 - Identity 4.1.1b|Identity 4.1.1b]], [[Ch05-S01-H06-B01 - Identity 5.1.6a|Identity 5.1.6a]].
