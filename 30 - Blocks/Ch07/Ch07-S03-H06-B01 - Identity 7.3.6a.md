# Linear Operator Identity 7.3.6a

**线性算子恒等式 7.3.6a** · identity · 7.3.6a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch07-S03-H06 - Proof and Consequences Linear Operator|Proof and Consequences: Linear Operator]] · [[Ch07-S03-H06 - Proof and Consequences Linear Operator|线性算子：证明与推论]]

The pairing is computed in coordinates, which gives the identity we quote later:

$$
\langle x, y \rangle \;=\; \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i \overline{y_i}
 \;=\; \lVert P_{M} x \rVert^{2} + \lVert P_{M^{\perp}} x \rVert^{2}.
$$

恒等式在正交投影的讨论中反复使用。
