# Sample Complexity Estimate 7.3.1c

**样本复杂度估计 7.3.1c** · estimate · 7.3.1c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch07-S03-H01 - Definition Sample Complexity|Definition: Sample Complexity]] · [[Ch07-S03-H01 - Definition Sample Complexity|样本复杂度：定义]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。

Used together with: [[Ch01-S04-H04-B02 - Proposition 1.4.4b|Proposition 1.4.4b]], [[Ch03-S01-H03-B02 - Example 3.1.3b|Example 3.1.3b]].
