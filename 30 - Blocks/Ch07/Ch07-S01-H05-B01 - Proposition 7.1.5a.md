# Entropy Bound Proposition 7.1.5a

**熵界命题 7.1.5a** · statement · 7.1.5a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch07-S01-H05 - Main Theorem Entropy Bound|Main Theorem: Entropy Bound]] · [[Ch07-S01-H05 - Main Theorem Entropy Bound|熵界：主定理]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。

Used together with: [[Ch08-S02-H02-B03 - Example 8.2.2c|Example 8.2.2c]], [[Ch09-S03-H01-B03 - Estimate 9.3.1c|Estimate 9.3.1c]].
