# Kernel Matrix Example 7.2.6c

**核矩阵例子 7.2.6c** · example · 7.2.6c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch07-S02-H06 - Proof and Consequences Kernel Matrix|Proof and Consequences: Kernel Matrix]] · [[Ch07-S02-H06 - Proof and Consequences Kernel Matrix|核矩阵：证明与推论]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。
