# Regularization Example 7.2.4a

**正则化例子 7.2.4a** · example · 7.2.4a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch07-S02-H04 - Key Lemma Regularization|Key Lemma: Regularization]] · [[Ch07-S02-H04 - Key Lemma Regularization|正则化：关键引理]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。

Used together with: [[Ch09-S04-H03-B03 - Proposition 9.4.3c|Proposition 9.4.3c]], [[Ch02-S01-H02-B02 - Estimate 2.1.2b|Estimate 2.1.2b]].
