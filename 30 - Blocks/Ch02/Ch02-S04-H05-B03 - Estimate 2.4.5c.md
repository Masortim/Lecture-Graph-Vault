# Dual Space Estimate 2.4.5c

**对偶空间估计 2.4.5c** · estimate · 2.4.5c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch02-S04-H05 - Main Theorem Dual Space|Main Theorem: Dual Space]] · [[Ch02-S04-H05 - Main Theorem Dual Space|对偶空间：主定理]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。
