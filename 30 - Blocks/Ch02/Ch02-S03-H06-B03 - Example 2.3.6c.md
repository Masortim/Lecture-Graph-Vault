# Linear Operator Example 2.3.6c

**线性算子例子 2.3.6c** · example · 2.3.6c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch02-S03-H06 - Proof and Consequences Linear Operator|Proof and Consequences: Linear Operator]] · [[Ch02-S03-H06 - Proof and Consequences Linear Operator|线性算子：证明与推论]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。

Used together with: [[Ch04-S02-H04-B02 - Proposition 4.2.4b|Proposition 4.2.4b]], [[Ch05-S03-H03-B02 - Example 5.3.3b|Example 5.3.3b]].
