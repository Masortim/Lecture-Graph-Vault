# Lipschitz Continuity Example 2.2.2c

**利普希茨连续性例子 2.2.2c** · example · 2.2.2c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch02-S02-H02 - Notation Lipschitz Continuity|Notation: Lipschitz Continuity]] · [[Ch02-S02-H02 - Notation Lipschitz Continuity|利普希茨连续性：记号约定]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。

Used together with: [[Ch01-S02-H05-B03 - Estimate 1.2.5c|Estimate 1.2.5c]], [[Ch02-S03-H04-B02 - Proposition 2.3.4b|Proposition 2.3.4b]].
