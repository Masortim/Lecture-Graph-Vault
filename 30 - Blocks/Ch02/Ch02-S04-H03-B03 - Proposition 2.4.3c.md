# Orthogonal Projection Proposition 2.4.3c

**正交投影命题 2.4.3c** · statement · 2.4.3c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch02-S04-H03 - Basic Properties Orthogonal Projection|Basic Properties: Orthogonal Projection]] · [[Ch02-S04-H03 - Basic Properties Orthogonal Projection|正交投影：基本性质]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。

Used together with: [[Ch05-S02-H01-B02 - Identity 5.2.1b|Identity 5.2.1b]], [[Ch06-S02-H06-B01 - Identity 6.2.6a|Identity 6.2.6a]].
