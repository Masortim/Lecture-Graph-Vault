# Entropy Bound Example 2.1.5d

**熵界例子 2.1.5d** · example · 2.1.5d

⬆️ Part of [[Ch02-S01-H05 - Main Theorem Entropy Bound|Main Theorem: Entropy Bound]] · [[Ch02-S01-H05 - Main Theorem Entropy Bound|熵界：主定理]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。

Used together with: [[Ch09-S03-H02-B03 - Example 9.3.2c|Example 9.3.2c]], [[Ch01-S04-H01-B03 - Estimate 1.4.1c|Estimate 1.4.1c]].
