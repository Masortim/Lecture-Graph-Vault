# Gradient Descent Proposition 2.2.1a

**梯度下降命题 2.2.1a** · statement · 2.2.1a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch02-S02-H01 - Definition Gradient Descent|Definition: Gradient Descent]] · [[Ch02-S02-H01 - Definition Gradient Descent|梯度下降：定义]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。
