# Entropy Bound Proposition 2.1.5a

**熵界命题 2.1.5a** · statement · 2.1.5a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch02-S01-H05 - Main Theorem Entropy Bound|Main Theorem: Entropy Bound]] · [[Ch02-S01-H05 - Main Theorem Entropy Bound|熵界：主定理]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。

Used together with: [[Ch09-S02-H02-B03 - Example 9.2.2c|Example 9.2.2c]], [[Ch01-S03-H01-B03 - Estimate 1.3.1c|Estimate 1.3.1c]].
