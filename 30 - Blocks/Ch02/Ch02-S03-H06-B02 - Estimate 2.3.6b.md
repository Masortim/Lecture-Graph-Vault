# Linear Operator Estimate 2.3.6b

**线性算子估计 2.3.6b** · estimate · 2.3.6b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch02-S03-H06 - Proof and Consequences Linear Operator|Proof and Consequences: Linear Operator]] · [[Ch02-S03-H06 - Proof and Consequences Linear Operator|线性算子：证明与推论]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。

Used together with: [[Ch04-S02-H02-B02 - Estimate 4.2.2b|Estimate 4.2.2b]], [[Ch05-S03-H01-B02 - Identity 5.3.1b|Identity 5.3.1b]].
