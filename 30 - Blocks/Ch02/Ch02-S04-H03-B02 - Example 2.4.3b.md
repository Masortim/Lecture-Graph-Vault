# Orthogonal Projection Example 2.4.3b

**正交投影例子 2.4.3b** · example · 2.4.3b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch02-S04-H03 - Basic Properties Orthogonal Projection|Basic Properties: Orthogonal Projection]] · [[Ch02-S04-H03 - Basic Properties Orthogonal Projection|正交投影：基本性质]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。

Used together with: [[Ch05-S01-H05-B02 - Identity 5.1.5b|Identity 5.1.5b]], [[Ch06-S02-H04-B01 - Example 6.2.4a|Example 6.2.4a]].
