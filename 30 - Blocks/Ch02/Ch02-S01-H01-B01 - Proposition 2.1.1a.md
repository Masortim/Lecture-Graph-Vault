# Closed Graph Proposition 2.1.1a

**闭图像命题 2.1.1a** · statement · 2.1.1a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch02-S01-H01 - Definition Closed Graph|Definition: Closed Graph]] · [[Ch02-S01-H01 - Definition Closed Graph|闭图像：定义]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。

Used together with: [[Ch08-S01-H04-B03 - Identity 8.1.4c|Identity 8.1.4c]], [[Ch09-S02-H03-B03 - Proposition 9.2.3c|Proposition 9.2.3c]].
