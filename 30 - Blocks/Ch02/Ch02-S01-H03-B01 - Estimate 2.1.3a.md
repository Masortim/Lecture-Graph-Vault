# Approximation Error Estimate 2.1.3a

**逼近误差估计 2.1.3a** · estimate · 2.1.3a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch02-S01-H03 - Basic Properties Approximation Error|Basic Properties: Approximation Error]] · [[Ch02-S01-H03 - Basic Properties Approximation Error|逼近误差：基本性质]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。
