# Markov Chain Example 2.3.4a

**马尔可夫链例子 2.3.4a** · example · 2.3.4a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch02-S03-H04 - Key Lemma Markov Chain|Key Lemma: Markov Chain]] · [[Ch02-S03-H04 - Key Lemma Markov Chain|马尔可夫链：关键引理]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。
