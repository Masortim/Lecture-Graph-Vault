# Kernel Matrix Identity 2.2.6a

**核矩阵恒等式 2.2.6a** · identity · 2.2.6a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch02-S02-H06 - Proof and Consequences Kernel Matrix|Proof and Consequences: Kernel Matrix]] · [[Ch02-S02-H06 - Proof and Consequences Kernel Matrix|核矩阵：证明与推论]]

The pairing is computed in coordinates, which gives the identity we quote later:

$$
\langle x, y \rangle \;=\; \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i \overline{y_i}
 \;=\; \lVert P_{M} x \rVert^{2} + \lVert P_{M^{\perp}} x \rVert^{2}.
$$
