# Closed Graph Estimate 2.1.1c

**闭图像估计 2.1.1c** · estimate · 2.1.1c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch02-S01-H01 - Definition Closed Graph|Definition: Closed Graph]] · [[Ch02-S01-H01 - Definition Closed Graph|闭图像：定义]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。
