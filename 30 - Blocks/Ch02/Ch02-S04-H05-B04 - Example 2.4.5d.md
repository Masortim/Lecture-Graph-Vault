# Dual Space Example 2.4.5d

**对偶空间例子 2.4.5d** · example · 2.4.5d

⬆️ Part of [[Ch02-S04-H05 - Main Theorem Dual Space|Main Theorem: Dual Space]] · [[Ch02-S04-H05 - Main Theorem Dual Space|对偶空间：主定理]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。
