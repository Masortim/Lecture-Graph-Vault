# Closed Graph Example 2.1.1d

**闭图像例子 2.1.1d** · example · 2.1.1d

⬆️ Part of [[Ch02-S01-H01 - Definition Closed Graph|Definition: Closed Graph]] · [[Ch02-S01-H01 - Definition Closed Graph|闭图像：定义]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。
