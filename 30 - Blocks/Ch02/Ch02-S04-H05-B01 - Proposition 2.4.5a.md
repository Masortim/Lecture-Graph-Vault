# Dual Space Proposition 2.4.5a

**对偶空间命题 2.4.5a** · statement · 2.4.5a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch02-S04-H05 - Main Theorem Dual Space|Main Theorem: Dual Space]] · [[Ch02-S04-H05 - Main Theorem Dual Space|对偶空间：主定理]]

For every $x \in X$ the quantity below is finite and controls the rest of the section:

$$
\lVert Tx \rVert \;\le\; L\,\lVert x \rVert_{X}, \qquad L = \sup_{\lVert x \rVert_{X}=1} \lVert Tx \rVert_{Y}.
$$

该命题是本节后续结果的出发点。
