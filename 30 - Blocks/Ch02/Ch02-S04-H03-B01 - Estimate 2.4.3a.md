# Orthogonal Projection Estimate 2.4.3a

**正交投影估计 2.4.3a** · estimate · 2.4.3a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch02-S04-H03 - Basic Properties Orthogonal Projection|Basic Properties: Orthogonal Projection]] · [[Ch02-S04-H03 - Basic Properties Orthogonal Projection|正交投影：基本性质]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。
