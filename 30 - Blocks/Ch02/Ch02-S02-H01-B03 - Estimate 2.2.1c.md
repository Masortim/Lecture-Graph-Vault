# Gradient Descent Estimate 2.2.1c

**梯度下降估计 2.2.1c** · estimate · 2.2.1c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch02-S02-H01 - Definition Gradient Descent|Definition: Gradient Descent]] · [[Ch02-S02-H01 - Definition Gradient Descent|梯度下降：定义]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。
