# Duality Gap Estimate 2.2.3a

**对偶间隙估计 2.2.3a** · estimate · 2.2.3a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch02-S02-H03 - Basic Properties Duality Gap|Basic Properties: Duality Gap]] · [[Ch02-S02-H03 - Basic Properties Duality Gap|对偶间隙：基本性质]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。
