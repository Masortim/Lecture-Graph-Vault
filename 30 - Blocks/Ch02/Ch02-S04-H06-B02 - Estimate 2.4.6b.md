# Adjoint Operator Estimate 2.4.6b

**伴随算子估计 2.4.6b** · estimate · 2.4.6b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch02-S04-H06 - Proof and Consequences Adjoint Operator|Proof and Consequences: Adjoint Operator]] · [[Ch02-S04-H06 - Proof and Consequences Adjoint Operator|伴随算子：证明与推论]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。

Used together with: [[Ch06-S01-H03-B02 - Example 6.1.3b|Example 6.1.3b]], [[Ch07-S02-H02-B01 - Identity 7.2.2a|Identity 7.2.2a]].
