# Uniform Boundedness Estimate 2.1.2b

**一致有界性估计 2.1.2b** · estimate · 2.1.2b

⬆️ Part of [[Ch02-S01-H02 - Notation Uniform Boundedness|Notation: Uniform Boundedness]] · [[Ch02-S01-H02 - Notation Uniform Boundedness|一致有界性：记号约定]]

A standard symmetrisation argument yields the quantitative bound:

$$
\mathbb{P}\bigl\{\,|f(X)-\mathbb{E}f| \ge t\,\bigr\}
 \;\le\; 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{2nt^{2}}{(b-a)^{2}}\right),
 \qquad t>0.
$$

此估计决定了样本复杂度的阶。
