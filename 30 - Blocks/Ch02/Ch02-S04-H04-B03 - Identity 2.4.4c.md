# Basis and Coordinates Identity 2.4.4c

**基与坐标恒等式 2.4.4c** · identity · 2.4.4c

⬆️ Part of [[Ch02-S04-H04 - Key Lemma Basis and Coordinates|Key Lemma: Basis and Coordinates]] · [[Ch02-S04-H04 - Key Lemma Basis and Coordinates|基与坐标：关键引理]]

The pairing is computed in coordinates, which gives the identity we quote later:

$$
\langle x, y \rangle \;=\; \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i \overline{y_i}
 \;=\; \lVert P_{M} x \rVert^{2} + \lVert P_{M^{\perp}} x \rVert^{2}.
$$

恒等式在正交投影的讨论中反复使用。
