# Regularization Example 2.2.4a

**正则化例子 2.2.4a** · example · 2.2.4a

⬆️ Part of [[Ch02-S02-H04 - Key Lemma Regularization|Key Lemma: Regularization]] · [[Ch02-S02-H04 - Key Lemma Regularization|正则化：关键引理]]

The model case below shows that the hypothesis cannot be dropped:

$$
T e_k \;=\; \lambda_k e_k, \qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} |\lambda_k| \;<\; \infty,
 \qquad \lVert T \rVert_{\mathrm{tr}} = \sum_{k} |\lambda_k|.
$$

去掉条件后结论不再成立。
